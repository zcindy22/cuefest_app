Public festival guidance used as input (paraphrased from public coverage and the festival FAQ).
Sources: BizBash coverage of III Points, and the official III Points guide at iiipoints.com/guide. No personal data, no social media data.

- Wristband pickup was slow on Friday night, so a digital pass and arriving early help.
- Heavy rain and a midnight storm pushed thousands indoors, so rain gear and weather pauses are likely.
- The main entrance got congested, so extra time to get in is wise.
- Attendance hit about 80,000, so lines are likely and groups should pick a meetup spot.
- The festival FAQ says in and out re-entry is not allowed, so attendees should bring what they need before going in.
- Official bag rules: clear bags can be up to 12 by 6 by 12 inches, and clutches up to 6 by 9 inches, clear or not.
- Official bag rules: non clear backpacks and purses are not allowed, and all bags can be searched at entry or at any point.
- Official bag rules: hydration packs are allowed with up to two main compartments and one smaller one, and the bladder must be empty at entry.
- The official guide lists outside food and drinks, glass, cans, coolers, chairs, blankets, tents, drones and professional camera gear as not allowed.
